# Changes

- **3 November 2026, the same file name.** *A teacher's own file*: Maktab takes a file as the newer version only of
  the file he sent before under the same name; a file of another name only adds, so two files for one sub-topic keep
  both. Keep his file's name on your copy.
- **3 November 2026, how far you have got.** *A teacher's own file*, *Say how far you have got*: your AI names each
  chapter as it starts it, *Chapter 3 of 7: Chemical bonding*, and ends each document with a short summary: what it
  made, what it removed, what it wrote itself and what it could not do, a count on each line. Both show where you
  watch it work, in the terminal or the chat; the lists stay in its note and under *Changes made by AI*.
- **2 November 2026, wide tables on a phone.** *A teacher's own file*, *Lists, pictures, tables*: on a phone, Maktab
  draws a wide table whose rows each describe one thing in words as a card per row, and every other wide table scrolls
  sideways; it decides from the cells, so tables stay unmarked. Only a table of words that must be read across gets
  the line `[keep as table]` on its own just above it, which keeps it a table and is never shown to students.
  *A Maktab topic file*: the export writes that line above a table so marked; delete it to let a phone show a table of words as
  cards.
- **2 November 2026, Start here and Wrap up.** *A teacher's own file*, *The shape* and *Start here and Wrap up*: a
  sub-topic may open with a Heading 2 *Start here* (what it is about, what to know first) and close with a Heading 2
  *Wrap up* (his summary and key points), each left out where he has nothing for it. Students read a sub-topic one
  part at a time, and Maktab shows these two as its first and last screens. Common mistakes under *Wrap up* are copied
  word for word only from his misconception boxes, never from *Examiner's advice* or *Top tip*, which often hold the
  right answer.
- **2 November 2026, the Past paper questions tab.** A sub-topic's last tab is now named **Past paper questions**, as
  each entry on it is one question (M-500). *A teacher's own file*: the questions under his past-paper heading go to
  its Past paper questions tab. *A topic file*: its *Past papers* heading stays as it is; *Past paper questions* reads
  the same. No file needs changing.
- **2 November 2026, a multiple-choice question's pictures.** *A teacher's own file*, *Past-paper questions*, 5: a
  multiple-choice question needs no `Words:` under its pictures. Maktab shows the picture and leaves its words out, so a
  student never reads its statements or options twice; its own lines and its typed options (`A⇥1 and 2`) come in as
  before. A structured question's and a mark scheme's `Words:` are unchanged.
- **2 November 2026, part titles, suggested answers and what could mislead.** *A teacher's own file*: each Heading 2
  is a real title of 3 to 8 words saying what its part teaches, his heading where it says so, else yours, marked
  `[check: title written by AI; his heading was "…"]`. A question with no answer gets a `Suggested answer:` line,
  never an `Answer:`, and students are not asked it until he uses it; an unreadable word in an answer is marked `[?]`.
  What could teach a student something wrong (a diagram explained differently from the picture, a question that
  needed a missing picture, a sentence confidently wrong by standard AS & A Level science) is marked
  `[could mislead: why]`, a suggestion only, his words unchanged. In a table, a marker goes inside its cell. Maktab
  shows each marker to him as *Please check*. References to a question bank (`(Application: 3.3 bank Q112.)`) are
  deleted and listed, as book-only lines are. An outcome you chose stands, unmarked and unlisted. Say how far you
  have got, in small named batches, and never stop the run for one failure.
- **2 November 2026, a newer version replaces.** *A teacher's own file*: Maktab takes his newer file as the new
  version of what his last one brought in (M-488), so a part missing from the copy is taken out of his class. Keep
  all of his file, every time he sends it again.
- **2 November 2026, mark schemes word for word.** *A teacher's own file*: a mark scheme's `Words:` are its words
  exactly as Cambridge printed them: keep each `;` and `/`, its notes (`R`, `A`, `I`,
  `AW`, `ecf`, `max`), its brackets and its marks, and underline every word it underlines. Maktab reads them into
  points (the Mark Schemes Spec); a word changed or an underline lost changes how an answer is marked.
- **1 November 2026, tables in pictures.** *A teacher's own file*: never type a table drawn in a question's picture
  into its `Words:`, neither as rows nor with `|` between its cells: the picture shows it, so write only its title,
  *Table 1.1*, where it stands, and go on with the words after it. Maktab now leaves out a number or part label at the
  start of a question's words that repeats its header's (*2 The high blood pressure…* under *Question 2(b)*).
- **31 October 2026, the Past papers tab.** *A teacher's own file*: a sub-topic's past-paper questions go to its
  **Past papers tab**, the last of its page's tabs, where students answer them and check them. A structured question
  needs its mark scheme, and a picture of the question or of its mark scheme its `Words:` under it, for both. The
  heading and notes over them stay out of the lesson text, as before: the questions under them are on the tab.
- **31 October 2026, books.** *A teacher's own file*: delete every line that only points to a printed book
  (`Coursebook: …`, `Read: …`, `Textbook reading: …`, `Compare: coursebook …`, a table of each sub-topic's pages), and
  list them in your note. The `Read:` line asked for on 28 October is withdrawn: Maktab shows the class's books beside
  each sub-topic and opens each at its page. Maktab also leaves such lines out of a file prepared before.
- **29 October 2026, past papers.** *A teacher's own file*: the heading and notes over a sub-topic's past-paper
  questions (*Exam practice 2.1 · …*, *Paper 1 · Multiple choice (6 questions)*, *All 6 … are included.*) stay out
  of its lesson text: its Past papers card lists the papers. They need no mark.
- **29 October 2026, keys.** *A teacher's own file*: the lines under his keys that say how his questions are marked
  (*Note: Questions marked ⚠ …*, *How the tags are assigned: …*, a key to multiple-completion answers and the lines
  over and under it) stay out with the keys, before the first sub-topic; they need no mark.
- **29 October 2026, gaps.** *A teacher's own file*: every short question names an outcome of the sub-topic it sits
  in, never one of another sub-topic (Maktab files that under no outcome, in *Practise*). The code alone is enough:
  take it from the sub-topic's list in the export or on the course's page in *subjects/*, which has codes without
  their wording, and never leave the outcome off for want of its wording. A label line in his own style is kept
  exactly as he wrote it, his words never moved between label and title. `Changes made by AI` goes last, after
  `Summary` only where his file has one: never add an empty `Summary`.
- **29 October 2026, unsure.** *A teacher's own file*: where you are truly unsure of something you made, end its
  paragraph, box line, heading, picture's caption or table cell with `[check: <one plain reason>]` (not a worked
  example or a short question: list those in your note). Maktab leaves the marker off every
  lesson page and shows him the reason as a warning on what came in, until he presses *Looks right* or edits it. A
  box with no label line, or a `Box · ` box with his title, comes in with a warning to check its type; a labelled box
  with none.
- **29 October 2026, marks.** *A teacher's own file*: never delete a part only paper needs: put it in a one-cell box
  whose first line is `Print only · <what it is>` (the cover, the colour key, *How to use these notes*, a form, a *Take a
  break* box), which Maktab leaves out (*Marked for print only.*); a table of the sub-topics and a line to fill in need
  no mark. End the copy with a Heading 1 `Changes made by AI` listing what you could
  not keep as he had it (a PDF converted to Word, a table rebuilt, a paragraph split); Maktab keeps the list with the
  import and shows it to the teacher, never on a lesson page. The note no longer lists your changes. A PDF made Word is checked
  for its equations, sub- and superscripts, tables and scanned pages, each part rebuilt listed there.
- **29 October 2026, stamp.** *A teacher's own file*: put one line in the copy's page header, `Prepared for Maktab by
  <you and your model>, <day>, following the directions <version>`, the version the one line in `VERSION`, written at
  each publish. Maktab keeps the stamp with the import and never shows it on a lesson page.
- **29 October 2026, last.** *A teacher's own file*: leave his cover, colour key, Bloom's table and *How to use these
