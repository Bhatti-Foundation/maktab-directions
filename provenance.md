# Where material comes from

A college may publish only material it has the right to use. **When she imports a file, Maktab
asks her where its new material came from**: *Written by me*, *Free to use: I checked its
licence*, *Not known* or *Not licensed*. *Not licensed* stays out; *Not known* comes in but cannot
be published until she answers. Each piece keeps who imported it.

She answers once per file, so **your job is to make that answer true**:

1. **Her own writing goes in the topic file.** Her notes, her worksheets, her quizzes.
2. **Anything she did not write stays out of that file**: a page copied from a textbook, a
   published paper's questions, another teacher's notes, a website's text. List each in your note
   with where it came from.
3. **If she wants such material in**, put it in a separate copy of her export, and say in your note
   where each piece came from and what its licence says, if you can find it. She then answers for
   that file on its own.
4. **When you cannot tell** whether she wrote something, treat it as not hers and say so.
5. **A link is not a copy.** A video or web page she saved goes in your note to add on the page,
   whoever made it.

Maktab cannot check a licence for you, and these directions cannot make you follow them. They ask
you to, and her answer at import is recorded.
